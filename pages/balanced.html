Balanced Transportation Problems

A transportation problem is balanced when total supply is equal to total demand.

Total Supply = Total Demand

Example

SourceSupply
D100
E300
F300
Total700

DestinationDemand
A300
B200
C200
Total700
700 = 700. This is a balanced transportation problem.

Important Characteristics

  • No dummy source is required.
  • No dummy destination is required.
  • All supply and demand constraints can be satisfied together.
  • The model can proceed to an initial allocation method.
