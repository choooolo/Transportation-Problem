Transportation Problems Formula Reference

Total Transportation Cost

Z = ΣΣ cᵢⱼxᵢⱼ

Multiply allocations by unit costs and add them.

Balanced Transportation

Σ Supply = Σ Demand

Determines whether a problem is balanced.

Number of Basic Allocations

m + n − 1

Required number of independent basic cells in a non-degenerate basis.

VAM Penalty

Second Lowest − Lowest

Calculates row and column penalties.

MODI Potentials

uᵢ + vⱼ = cᵢⱼ

Used to determine U and V values.

MODI Opportunity Cost

Δᵢⱼ = cᵢⱼ − uᵢ − vⱼ

Used to test optimality for minimization.

Maximization Conversion

c'ᵢⱼ = M − pᵢⱼ

Converts a fixed-total profit problem into minimization.

Allocation Quantity

MIN(Supply, Demand)

Determines the maximum possible allocation.

Dummy Destination

Supply − Demand

Used when supply exceeds demand.

Dummy Source

Demand − Supply

Used when demand exceeds supply.
