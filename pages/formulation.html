Mathematical Formulation

Learn how to represent a transportation problem using mathematical equations.

Decision Variables

xᵢⱼ = Quantity transported from source i to destination j

Transportation Costs

cᵢⱼ = Cost per unit from source i to destination j

Objective Function

MIN Z = Σᵢ Σⱼ cᵢⱼxᵢⱼ

The objective is to minimize the total transportation cost.

Supply Constraints

Σⱼ xᵢⱼ = aᵢ

The total quantity shipped from each source must equal its supply in a balanced transportation model.

Demand Constraints

Σᵢ xᵢⱼ = bⱼ

The total quantity received by each destination must equal its demand.

Non-Negativity Constraints

xᵢⱼ ≥ 0

Meaning of Symbols

SymbolMeaning
xᵢⱼQuantity transported
cᵢⱼUnit transportation cost
aᵢSupply of source i
bⱼDemand of destination j
mNumber of sources
nNumber of destinations
ZTotal transportation cost
For a balanced transportation problem, total supply equals total demand.
