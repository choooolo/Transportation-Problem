Glossary

Transportation Problems Glossary

Search important terms used in Operations Research.

Allocation

Quantity assigned to a transportation route.

Basic Feasible Solution

An allocation satisfying all constraints with a valid independent basis.

Balanced

Total supply equals total demand.

Closed Loop

Alternating path used for transportation allocation adjustments.

Constraint

A restriction imposed on a mathematical model.

Degeneracy

Fewer than m+n−1 independent basic cells.

Demand

Required quantity at a destination.

Destination

Location receiving transported goods.

Dummy Source

Artificial source used when demand exceeds supply.

Dummy Destination

Artificial destination used when supply exceeds demand.

Feasible Solution

Solution that satisfies all constraints.

Least Cost

Method selecting the lowest available transportation cost.

MODI

Modified Distribution Method, also called U-V Method.

North-West Corner

Initial allocation method beginning at the top-left cell.

Objective Function

Mathematical expression to minimize or maximize.

Opportunity Cost

Estimated objective change when an unused route enters.

Penalty

Difference between the two lowest available costs in VAM.

Supply

Available quantity at a source.

Transportation Cost

Unit cost of moving goods along a route.

VAM

Vogel's Approximation Method.
