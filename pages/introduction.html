What is a Transportation Problem?

A Transportation Problem is a mathematical optimization problem that determines the most efficient way of transporting goods from multiple sources to multiple destinations while satisfying supply and demand requirements.

The main objective is usually to minimize the total transportation cost.

Purpose

Minimize Cost

Find the cheapest combination of shipping routes.

Meet Demand

Ensure every destination receives its required quantity.

Manage Supply

Distribute available goods without exceeding supply.

Real-World Applications

  • Manufacturing companies distributing products to warehouses.
  • Food suppliers delivering goods to supermarkets.
  • Hospitals distributing medical supplies.
  • Relief organizations distributing emergency resources.
  • Companies managing regional distribution centers.

Two Main Stages

1. Initial Basic Feasible Solution

Determine an initial allocation using North-West Corner, Least Cost, VAM, or another initial solution method.

2. Optimality Testing

Check whether the current allocation can be improved using MODI or Stepping Stone.

An initial feasible solution is not automatically an optimal solution.
