METHOD 02 · INITIAL ALLOCATION

Least Cost Method (LCM)

The Least Cost Method selects the available transportation route with the lowest unit transportation cost.

Procedure

  1. Find the lowest cost in the available transportation table.
  2. Allocate the maximum possible quantity to that cell.
  3. Subtract the allocation from the corresponding supply.
  4. Subtract the allocation from the corresponding demand.
  5. Cross out the exhausted row or satisfied column.
  6. Find the next lowest available cost.
  7. Repeat until all supply and demand are satisfied.

Allocation Formula

xᵢⱼ = MIN(Supplyᵢ, Demandⱼ)

Example

Available supply = 300

Destination demand = 200

Transportation cost = 3

Allocation = MIN(300, 200) = 200

The source has 100 units remaining, while the destination is already satisfied.

Handling Ties

If two or more cells have the same lowest transportation cost, use the tie-breaking rule required by your instructor. If none is specified, a consistent rule is to choose the cell allowing the largest allocation.

Total Cost

TC = Σ (Allocation × Unit Cost)
Remember: Least Cost considers transportation costs when selecting the next route, unlike North-West Corner.
