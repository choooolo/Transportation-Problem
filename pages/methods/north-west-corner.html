Initial Solution Methods
METHOD 01 · INITIAL ALLOCATION

North-West Corner Method (NWCM)

The North-West Corner Method is used to obtain an initial basic feasible solution by starting at the upper-left corner of the transportation table.

Procedure

  1. Start at the top-left cell.
  2. Compare the available supply and demand.
  3. Allocate the minimum of the two values.
  4. If supply is exhausted, move down to the next row.
  5. If demand is satisfied, move right to the next column.
  6. Continue until all supply and demand are satisfied.

Important Formula

Allocation = MIN(Remaining Supply, Remaining Demand)

Worked Example

Suppose the first source has supply of 100 and the first destination has demand of 300.

Allocation = MIN(100, 300) = 100

Source 1 is exhausted, so move down to the next row.

Advantages

  • Simple and easy to perform.
  • Quickly generates an initial feasible solution.
  • Useful for learning transportation table allocation.

Disadvantage

It ignores transportation costs when selecting cells, so the resulting initial cost may be high.
