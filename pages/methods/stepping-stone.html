Stepping Stone Method

The Stepping Stone Method determines whether an initial transportation solution can be improved by evaluating unused transportation routes.

Procedure

  1. Obtain an initial basic feasible solution.
  2. Identify an unoccupied cell.
  3. Start a closed loop from that cell.
  4. Move horizontally or vertically through occupied cells.
  5. Return to the original cell without repeating other cells.
  6. Assign alternating plus and minus signs.
  7. Calculate the net transportation cost change.
  8. Repeat for all eligible unoccupied cells.
  9. Choose the most negative improvement index.
  10. Adjust allocations using the smallest minus-position allocation.
  11. Repeat until no negative improvement index remains.

Opportunity Cost

Δ = Sum of Plus Costs − Sum of Minus Costs

Optimality Condition

For minimization, if every eligible unoccupied cell has an improvement index greater than or equal to zero, the solution is optimal.

Important Terms

  • Closed loop
  • Entering cell
  • Leaving cell
  • Plus position
  • Minus position
  • Improvement index
