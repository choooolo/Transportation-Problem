METHOD 03 · INITIAL ALLOCATION

Vogel's Approximation Method (VAM)

Vogel's Approximation Method uses penalties to determine which row or column should be allocated first.

What is a Penalty?

A penalty represents the difference between the two lowest available transportation costs in a row or column.

Penalty = Second Lowest Cost − Lowest Cost

Procedure

  1. Find the two lowest eligible costs in every active row.
  2. Subtract the lowest cost from the second lowest cost.
  3. Repeat the same process for every active column.
  4. Identify the row or column with the highest penalty.
  5. Choose the lowest-cost cell in that row or column.
  6. Allocate the maximum possible quantity.
  7. Update supply and demand.
  8. Remove exhausted rows and satisfied columns.
  9. Recalculate all penalties.
  10. Repeat until complete.

Example of Penalty

Suppose a row has available costs:

3, 5, 8

The two lowest costs are 3 and 5.

Penalty = 5 − 3 = 2

Important Reminder

Always recalculate the penalties after every allocation. Do not reuse the original penalty values.
