Special Cases

Dummy Sources and Destinations

Dummy Destination

Use a dummy destination when total supply is greater than total demand.

Dummy Demand = Total Supply − Total Demand

Dummy Source

Use a dummy source when total demand is greater than total supply.

Dummy Supply = Total Demand − Total Supply

Example

Supply = 800

Demand = 650

Dummy Destination = 800 − 650 = 150

Another example:

Supply = 500

Demand = 700

Dummy Source = 700 − 500 = 200
After adding the dummy row or column, total supply and demand should be equal.
