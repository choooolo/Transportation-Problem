Special Cases

Multiple Optimal Solutions

A transportation problem may have more than one allocation that produces the same optimal total cost.

How to Identify It

For a minimization problem, if an eligible non-basic cell has a reduced cost equal to zero at an optimal solution, an alternative optimal solution may exist.

Δᵢⱼ = 0

Important Points

  • The current solution is optimal.
  • An eligible unused route has zero reduced cost.
  • A valid closed-loop pivot may produce another allocation.
  • The total transportation cost remains unchanged.
Multiple optimal solutions have the same minimum objective value, even though their shipment allocations differ.
