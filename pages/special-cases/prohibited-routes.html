Special Cases

Prohibited Routes

A prohibited route is a transportation route where shipment is not permitted.

Reasons for Prohibited Routes

  • Road closures.
  • Unavailable transportation services.
  • Geographical restrictions.
  • Contractual restrictions.
  • Unavailable facilities.

Representation

Mark prohibited cells as unavailable or use a suitable very large penalty M when using a conventional mathematical formulation.

Never allocate goods to a prohibited route. A large M value must be selected carefully and must not be confused with a normal transportation cost.
