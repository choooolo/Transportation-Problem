How to Read the Table

  • Rows represent the sources.
  • Columns represent the destinations.
  • Numbers inside cells represent unit transportation costs.
  • The rightmost column represents supply.
  • The bottom row represents demand.
  • The bottom-right cell represents the total supply or total demand when balanced.
