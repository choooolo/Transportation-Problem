Unbalanced Transportation Problems

A transportation problem is unbalanced when total supply is not equal to total demand.

Total Supply ≠ Total Demand

Case 1: Supply Exceeds Demand

Example:

Supply = 800
Demand = 600
Difference = 200

Add a dummy destination with demand of 200.

Dummy Costs

Dummy routes usually receive a transportation cost of zero when there is no specified cost for unused supply or unmet demand.

If the problem specifies a shortage penalty, disposal cost, or emergency purchasing cost, use that amount instead of automatically assuming zero.
